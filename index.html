FAQ

Questions.

Do I need to give my Instagram password?

No. The app never asks for it and never connects to your account. It only reads the export files you download from Meta.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The files are read locally in your browser and are not sent to any server. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it work on my phone?

FollowTracker is designed for use on a computer with a modern browser.

Is it real-time?

No. Results reflect the export you load. To see changes, request a new export and compare it with a previous one.

Why is the number of accounts I follow different from my profile?

Meta's export can contain deactivated or removed accounts that Instagram no longer counts. The app shows what is in the file, so the number can be higher. The file doesn't say which ones they are; you can find them by opening the profiles from the "Verifica seguiti" section and excluding them.
