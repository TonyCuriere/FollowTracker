What it does

  • Mutual follows: accounts you follow that follow you back.
  • Not following back: accounts you follow that don't follow you back.
  • Pending requests: follow requests you sent that have not been answered.
  • History comparison: save several exports in the app and compare them over time (at least two different exports are needed).
  • Export and print: download results as CSV, or print and save the report as PDF from your browser.

How it works

  1. Request your data export (followers and following) from Meta. A step-by-step guide is included.
  2. Open FollowTracker.html on your computer and select the files you downloaded.
  3. Read the results. Nothing is uploaded: the analysis happens on your device.

Privacy by design

The app never asks for your Instagram password and never connects to your account. The files you select are read locally in your browser and are not sent to any server. The app also works offline. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Requirements and limits

  • Designed for use on a computer with a modern browser.
  • You need to request your data export from Meta first; preparation of the export can take some time.

  • Saved history stays in the browser you used and can be lost if you clear its data.
