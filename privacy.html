The FollowTracker app

FollowTracker is a file you open in your own browser. The Meta export files you select are read and analyzed locally on your device. They are not uploaded, transmitted to us or stored on any server of ours. The app does not ask for your Instagram password, does not connect to your Instagram account and does not include analytics or tracking.

If you use the history feature, comparisons are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
